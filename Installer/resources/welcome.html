Welcome to MatrixScreenSaver

This installs MatrixScreenSaver — an open-source Matrix-style screensaver with independent animation per display.

The screensaver is installed to /Library/Screen Savers/ and is available to all users on this Mac. Your admin password is required.

Gatekeeper note: If macOS blocked this installer when you opened it, right-click (Control-click) the .pkg and choose Open — then click Open in the confirmation dialog. Or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
